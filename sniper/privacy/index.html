Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Who We Are

Sniper ("Sniper", "we", "us") is a free mobile app where adult players challenge each other to snooker matches, climb the rankings, post and chat. Sniper does not involve betting, wagering or prize money. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: your username, email address, country, and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider in protected form, and we cannot see it.

Profile and activity: your matches, scores, results, ratings and reviews, challenges sent and received, ranking position, play coins balance and coin history, and the last time you were active in the app.

Content you create: your profile photo, posts, photos or videos you add to posts, comments, likes, and messages you send to other players or to Sniper.

Reports and blocks: reports you make about content or players, and players you block.

Device information: your push notification token, device type, and basic technical logs that help us fix problems.

Advertising information: our advertising partner Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and device details, and how you interact with ads (see section 4). We also count views and taps on sponsored ads shown in the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account and sign you in;
  • match you with opponents and run challenges, matches, rankings and ratings;
  • run play coins, including refills and rewards;
  • show ads and measure how they perform;
  • send notifications about challenges, matches and messages;
  • send account emails such as sign-up and password reset codes;
  • review reports, keep the app safe, fix bugs and improve it; and
  • meet our legal obligations.

4. Ads and Play Coins

Sniper is free and is supported by ads. We show ads from Google AdMob and sponsored ads placed directly by advertisers. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show ads and measure how they perform, under Google's own privacy policy.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your choice in a consent message before ads load, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

You can choose to watch a short rewarded video ad to earn play coins. Play coins are used only inside Sniper. They cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money, and they have no cash value.

5. Who We Share Information With

Other players: your username, country, ranking, win and loss record, ratings, reviews, posts and profile photo are visible to other players.

Service providers: companies that help us run the app, including cloud hosting and database services (Supabase), push notifications (Google Firebase), advertising (Google AdMob), image and video hosting (Cloudinary), and email delivery. They may only use your data to provide services to us.

Authorities: where the law requires it, or where it is needed to protect the safety and rights of our users or the app.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data and Deleting Your Account

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Profile, tap the gear icon, then tap Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and the data linked to it, such as your matches, ratings, posts, comments, messages and play coins.

Copies of photos or videos you uploaded may stay with our media host for a time, and backups and security logs may keep a copy for a limited period. Email us if you want a file removed sooner. We may keep records we need for safety, fraud prevention and legal requirements, for example reports about conduct.

If you cannot open the app, you can ask for deletion by email. See the account deletion page on our website.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

8. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and under the GDPR and UK GDPR if you are in Europe or the UK, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data, subject to legal record-keeping needs;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting earlier processing; and
  • complain to your data protection authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To use any of these rights, email us at peterwisdom490@yahoo.com.

9. Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications about challenges, matches and messages. We store a device token for this purpose. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

10. Age Limit

Sniper is for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we will close it and delete the associated data. If you believe a minor is using the app, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email us at peterwisdom490@yahoo.com.
