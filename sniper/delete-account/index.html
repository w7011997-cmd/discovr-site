Delete your Snipers Pool account

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Snipers Pool and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap the gear icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

If you have a match in progress, finish or forfeit it first, then try again.

What is deleted

Your account and the data linked to it are permanently removed, including your profile, username and sign-in details, matches, ratings and reviews, posts, comments, likes, messages, play coins and notification settings. This cannot be undone.

What may be kept

Copies of photos or videos you uploaded may stay with our media host for a time, and backups and security logs may keep a copy for a limited period. We may keep records we need for safety, fraud prevention and legal requirements, for example reports about conduct. Email us if you want a file removed sooner.
